## 1. Scope / Trigger

Read before editing `task-execution-storage.ts`, `task-amendment.ts`, `task-admission.ts` or the initial certification tail in `task-store.ts`.
This is unaccepted G3 engineering, not executor or production integration.
`src/index.ts` remains unchanged.
Apply has no independent G4 evidence provider and always refuses; its negative port is not complete apply admission.

## 2. Signatures

- `TaskStore.evolveExecutionShell(): void`: explicit checked additive evolution,
  no certified rows and no observation/replay migration.

  authority is checked but absent/JSON evidence never permits mutation.

## 3. Contracts

`TASK_EXECUTION_SCHEMA` adds four STRICT tables: singleton, content-addressed immutable bytes, revisions and selected heads.
Complete G3 is a checked additive shell catalog profile (including both original supporting-index profiles), not an alteration to G1/G2 tables or a new private root.
Foundation-only readers continue rejecting richer catalogs.
Existing-only opening never migrates.

Original raw JSON/BLOB bytes and metadata are SQL-byte-gated before hydration, fatally decoded, canonically hashed and joined to the original G2 approval/interaction/operation/event and prepared/material horizon.
Initial certification requires exact module-private genuine receipt membership, not domain/compiler strings.
Its token retains the final compilation scope; TaskStore authenticates exact original borrowed work/checkout before I/O and independently reobserves inputs after callbacks.
The genuine session omits its redundant legacy compiler-observation callback only on this route; any supplied callback and all non-G3/fixture paths retain their old contract.
Pure terminal service consumption revokes the original genuine receipt membership.
Original witnesses/files and pure receipt/response checks precede callback-free approval/revision/head writes.
Ambiguous COMMIT supplies no outcome seal or execution permit.

Each revision is at most 64 KiB, each immutable plan at most 16 MiB, and the per-task revision sequence at most 4096.
Amendments also consume the original ordinary operation horizon, including retained terminal reservations.
Initial rows are already counted by their G2 issuance operations.
Operation IDs exclude G2 operations/reserved keys.
Parent/current-head CAS and unique parent/sequence keys reject forks and replay; bytes commit with their reference.

Selected authority validates the full affected certified parent chain, not unrelated tasks or every historical ticket.
Explicit audit uses bounded task-ID pages, also checking orphan bytes and historical original issuers.
A genuinely new terminal review may append a new initial generation; the invalidated former approval, preparation and full parent chain remain immutable.
The old generation is not executable merely because its history is intact.

Finite technical proofs compare complete verifier obligations (including ordered children/Red expectations), original baselines, unit objectives, roots, dependencies, conflicts/resources, outputs and all budgets.
Only unchanged candidate repair, same-unit phase read/write/delete repairs within captured scope, obligation-preserving IDs, fresh bound inputs and independent order changes are certifiable.
Structural malformed candidates return review-needed before native observation; native observation/close errors are never downgraded.

ID refresh preserves original recovery keys and maximum attempts.
Changed units or observed inputs invalidate all unit evidence conservatively, including consumers; `invalidateGlobalEvidence` explicitly invalidates cumulative/baseline, post-apply and other global evidence too.
A manifest/lock raw-byte change needs new review because old hashes cannot prove unchanged dependency surfaces.
These are invalidation requirements, not independently run reports or spent engine counters.
G4 must join its retained spending and apply invalidation; G3 does not invent execution facts or refund budgets.

Launch independently reacquires selected local facts, reobserves actual workspace/material/configuration/runner/profile/environment identities, repeats original head/bytes and ends in pure original-family guards.
Captured TaskStore methods/private fields reject duck-typed store substitutes.
Fresh attachment never rewrites original approval session/epoch/instance provenance.
The port returns no foreground permit and is not called by Open/history/native replay.
