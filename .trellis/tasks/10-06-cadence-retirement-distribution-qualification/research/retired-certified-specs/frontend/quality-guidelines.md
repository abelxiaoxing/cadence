## Lint and generation

Both generated worker/operator bundles are rebuilt by the canonical builder from the package cwd and checked byte-for-byte.
Markdown uses one sentence per line, aligned tables, `1.` ordered lists and bounded sanctioned HTML only.
The old prompt HTML exception and upstream JSON override are removed, not replaced by a broad lint exemption.
