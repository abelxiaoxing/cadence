## Acceptance Criteria

- **W6-AC1:** Real browser-only workflow plans/starts subagents, inspects details, confirms checks and records review on the common owner.
- **W6-AC2:** Substantial upstream modules/tests are directly adapted with provenance, not replaced by a thin dashboard.
- **W6-AC3:** Tool/message/timeline/diff/history/search/export remain usable beyond loaded DOM and realtime buffers.
- **W6-AC4:** Desktop/phone actions and Cancel are accessible and show stale/unknown/failed/capture-coverage facts.
- **W6-AC5:** No generic sender, SDK runtime, transcript repair, auto-resume, plugin/PTY/Git mutation or persistent browser token arrives through reuse.

## Out of Scope

- Partial-product release or deferring core scope.
- Unrelated dirty files, archive/consumer cleanup, credential export or automatic Git/install/publication.
- Competing execution owners or duplicate protocol/projection rules.
- Runtime success claims based only on source inspection or mocks.
